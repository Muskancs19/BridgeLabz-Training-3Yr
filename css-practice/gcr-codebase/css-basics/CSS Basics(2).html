History of Paris

Paris is the capital of France and one of the world's most famous cities. It has a long history, beautiful architecture and rich cultural traditions.

Famous Places

The Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum and Notre-Dame are some of the most famous attractions in Paris.

Parisian Culture

Paris is known for art, fashion, food, literature and music. Visitors can experience its unique atmosphere throughout the city.
